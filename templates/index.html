Amazon's Self-Service Portal for Managing Prime Video Channels

Safe and Secure

Telescope separates users from admins; anyone can browse the channel table, but admin checks are implemented at the app routing level to ensure non-admins are never given access to backend operations even if they formulate their own POST request.

Validation Where it Matters

Telescope checks all submitted names and prices before updating the database to strip whitespace and maintain appropriate formatting, while psycopg2 prevents SQL injections so all data remains safe.

Safety Without Restriction

Telescope follows the NIST SP 800-63B guidelines when registering new users. Passwords must be longer than 15 characters, and no knowledge-based hints or counterproductive composition rules are enforced. All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before being stored, so private data is never exposed.

Simple but Effective Logging and Monitoring

Every failed login attempt, missed database connection, invalid input, and access control violation is securely logged, allowing Telescope to easily evolve through quicker development.

Clear to Read, Easy to Understand

Telescope's channel table can be sorted by any heading in both directions, even though free channels are displayed as "Free" instead of "£0.00" for readability. The page features simple colours with intentional contrast between the background and text. Every success, invalid input, and access error is clearly displayed using colour coded flash messages.
